You are summarising one RPA guidance document for a caseworker deciding
whether it is the document they need.

The document is given below as Markdown, converted from Word. It may carry
inline HTML, tables rendered as pipe rows, and blockquotes where the original
had bordered boxes. Ignore the conversion and read the guidance.

Return two things.

**about** — what the document is about: the subject matter it covers, the
schemes, claim types or systems it deals with, and the scope it sets for
itself. Two or three sentences.

**used_for** — what the document is used for: the task it supports, the point
in a process at which someone reaches for it, and who that person is. Two or
three sentences.

**keywords** — the terms someone would search by to reach this document.
Include, in this order:

1. **The initialism of the document's own name, and of the case type or check
   it handles** — the first letters of the words a colleague would run
   together when naming it out loud. "Revenue Option Claim Rule" is ROCR;
   "Land Use or Land Cover" is LULC; "Final Payment Case Check" is FPCC.
   Give it *even where the document never writes it*: it is what people call
   the thing, and it is the one term a search will miss otherwise. Give the
   phrase it is formed from as well, so both spellings find the document.
2. **The acronyms the document itself uses**, as it writes them, and their
   expansions where it defines them.
3. The schemes, systems, case types, option codes and checks it names.

Rules:

- Write plain English, in the third person, with no preamble and no heading.
- Say what this document covers that a sibling document would not. "Guidance
  for processing claims" describes half the corpus and helps nobody.
- Name the schemes, checks and systems the document itself names, including
  acronyms as it writes them, so that searching for one finds this document.
- Describe only what the document contains. If it does not say who uses it or
  when, say what can be told from its content and no more — do not infer a
  process the document does not describe.
